01 / About

Understanding before using.

I am most at home when I can trace a system back to its foundations — whether that is a theorem in approximation theory, the architecture of an AI agent, or the dynamics of a financial market.

I resist reducing myself to a single job title. For me, mathematics offers structure, engineering offers craft, markets offer a living complex system, and philosophy — especially Nietzsche’s insistence on independent judgment and Selbstüberwindung — keeps the compass honest.

Outside of study, I enjoy quiet travel in Japan, tinkering with small hardware and personal tools, and exploring virtual spaces.
