01 · About

Curious, analytical, unfinished.

I prefer understanding how a system is built over merely using it. Whether studying stochastic processes, designing software and AI agents, or watching financial markets, I start from first principles and look for the underlying structure.

I don’t see these interests as separate careers or labels. Mathematics brings rigor; computer science brings craft; markets test ideas against reality; philosophy keeps the questions honest.

Away from the desk, I care about quiet travel — especially walking through Kyoto and Tokyo — small hardware experiments, and the spaces we inhabit digitally.

02 · Philosophy

Selbstüberwindung & independence.

Friedrich Nietzsche’s writing has quietly shaped how I think about judgment and growth — not as slogans, but as a discipline of questioning inherited values and refusing intellectual inertia.

Ideas like Werden (becoming) and Amor Fati remind me that clarity is never finished. To re-examine one’s own assumptions is simply part of being awake.
